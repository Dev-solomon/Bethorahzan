01 — THE VISION

A place to belong.
A life to become.

Bethorahzan is a Christ-governed community devoted to feeding the body, soul and spirit. It exists to form people of character, wisdom and faith, while answering the practical needs of those facing hardship.

We convene for teaching, prayer, fellowship, feasting and purposeful gatherings. We also believe in tangible action: standing with orphanages, widows, individuals and communities confronting food insecurity or extreme poverty.

Membership is an invitation to participate in a shared vision, not simply to attend events. Members can connect through gatherings, community opportunities and requests for spiritual or practical support.

Our guiding Scripture: “And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and man.” — Luke 2:52 (KJV)
THE GOVERNANCE

The office of the Admiral

Bethorahzan is headed by the Admiral of Bethorahzan, supported by the Council of Twelve Generals, with its leadership entrusted to govern according to the principles of Christ.

Its authority is rooted in stewardship, service, accountability, wisdom and care for the community.

Growth in 3 Dimensions

The body, soul and spirit are interconnected. Our mandate is to cultivate flourishing in all three.
