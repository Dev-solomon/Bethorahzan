Information collected

We collect registration information, contact details, member preferences, appointment requests, event registrations, purchases, giving records and work applications to administer Bethorahzan programs.

Restricted information

Personal counseling, spiritual care and wellness requests are sensitive. Access should be restricted to authorized care staff, with operational retention limits and an incident response plan before launch. Do not submit emergency or extensive clinical information through general forms.

Payments

Payment details are collected and processed directly by Stripe. Bethorahzan maintains transaction references and status records to reconcile orders, donations and marketplace payments.

Your requests

Contact the community office to request an update, access review or deletion of personal information where applicable, subject to legal and accounting retention obligations.

Community principles

Members agree to respect one another and the community's Christ-centered mission, including dignity, honesty, safeguarding, and responsible participation in events, programs and communications.

Appointments and guidance

All appointments are requests until confirmed. Prayer, counseling and prophetic guidance are spiritual/community services, not medical, mental health or legal services. Seek qualified professional assistance when needed.

Purchases and donations

Shop purchases and contributions are processed through Stripe. The return/refund policy for books, digital goods and donations must be finalized before launch. No tax-deductibility is promised.

Work opportunities

Project owners fund a budget through Stripe, with a disclosed 10% Bethorahzan service fee and 90% proposed worker compensation. Publishing requires review; payment release requires administrator verification and a connected payout account. Unassigned projects may qualify for a full refund before assignment. The platform is not a licensed escrow or bank, and availability depends on applicable laws and payment provider approval.

Acceptable use

No fraudulent listings, exploitative hiring, illegal services, hate, harassment or abusive conduct. Bethorahzan may decline or remove listings and suspend accounts when justified under published policies.
